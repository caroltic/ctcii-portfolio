# User research 

## Target audience

The primary target audience for this project includes current dog owners, people considering getting a dog, and international students considering raising a dog while studying in the United States. I identified these audiences based on my personal experience as a Chinese American who owns a dog, started as a college student, and needs to bring my dog for travel across countries.

The intended audience is directly reflected in each section of the project. The exercise and calorie intake sections are designed for dog owners and future owners who want to understand the daily health responsibilities required to keep a dog physically healthy and nutritionally balanced. The annual cost comparison section targets students and young adults who must actively budget their expenses and evaluate whether dog ownership is financially realistic at their current stage in life. The veterinary care comparison between China and the United States speaks directly to international students by revealing how differences in healthcare systems, environment, and weather factors for pets can significantly impact how often and why dogs receive medical care.

## Representative Interviewees:

When identifying representative individuals to interview, I focused on three main groups that directly reflect my target audience. The first group includes friends who currently own dogs, since they can speak from real experience about daily routines, costs, feeding, and vet care. The second group includes classmates and friends who have talked about wanting to own a dog, as they represent future dog owners who are still forming expectations and guessing about responsibility and commitments. The third group includes my international student friends who are interested in getting a dog while studying in the United States and are also thinking about bringing their future dog back to their home country.

## Interview script

1. Do you currently have a dog, or are you thinking about getting one? Why or why not?

2. What do you think is or will be the most challenging part of owning a dog daily?

3. Before owning a dog (or thinking about getting one), what did you assume the main costs would be?

4. How important do you think daily walking and exercise are for a dog’s health? How often?

5. Do you ever think about how much your dog eats or how many calories they need scientifically? How do you determine how much to feed your dog?

6. If you are a student or international student, what concerns do you have about owning a dog while studying?

7. After seeing my project idea, early sketches, and visualizations, what part did you find the most interesting or surprising?

8. Is there anything you feel confused about, or that you'd like to see more clearly shown in my data visualizations?
